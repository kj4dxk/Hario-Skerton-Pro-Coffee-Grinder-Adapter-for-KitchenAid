# Hario Skerton Pro Coffee Grinder Adapter for KitchenAid

A custom adapter bracket for mounting a **Hario Skerton Pro coffee grinder** to a **KitchenAid stand mixer** for powered coffee grinding.

## Overview

The project adapts a manual coffee grinder for use with a stand mixer. The bracket positions and supports the Skerton Pro relative to the KitchenAid; the drive connection transfers rotation from the mixer to the grinder shaft.

## Compatibility

- **Grinder:** Hario Skerton Pro.
- **Mixer:** KitchenAid 6 Quart Bowl-Lift Stand Mixer.
- Compatibility with other Hario grinders has not been established.

## Design

The adapter bracket is designed in **SolidWorks**.

## Drive interfaces

| Component | Interface | Size |
|---|---|---|
| Hario Skerton Pro power input | Male hex | 7 mm |
| KitchenAid power output | Female square | 11.7 mm |

| 1 | KitchenAid 6 Quart Bowl-Lift Stand Mixer | Power source |
| 1 | Custom adapter bracket | Mounts grinder to mixer |
| 2 | 8″ zip ties | Assembly retention |
| 1 kit | 480Pcs 12 Values M2 M3 M4 Metric Hex Socket Cap Head Self Tapping Screw with Flat Gasket Washers Assortment Kit | Assembly screws and washers |

**Nut driver selection:** Magnetic nut drivers do not work for this adapter because their sockets are too shallow to engage enough of the Skerton Pro’s 7 mm hex shaft to transmit the required torque. Use a nonmagnetic 7 mm nut driver with sufficient socket depth.

The hardware kit is a source of fasteners; individual screw sizes and quantities used in the assembly are to be documented.
